# Style: presenter (a person talks about the product)

Back to [SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md). Status: **stub — not proven in runs.** Update with evidence after real runs.

## Known so far (untested here)

- The person needs a **character sheet** (face, hair, build, wardrobe) like the product does.
- Voice: a **video element** with recorded audio gives the model the voice and delivery. Higgsfield's creator
  records the lines themselves, even roughly; the human timing makes the AI voice feel alive.
- Seedance takes `audio_urls` (≤10) as reference audio.
- Dialogue in ACTION TIMING: quote the line, say who speaks, and give pauses room ("the pauses matter").
- Camera for talking: tripod-still or one slow, controlled move; mix medium and close.
