# Style: product-hero (no people, no voice)

Back to [SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md). Status: **proven in runs** (Seedance 2.0, 12 s remixes of a car-stunt ad
with a burrito and a mouse).

The product is the only character: a stunt, reveal or transformation. Sound is music and effects, no dialogue.
The camera and the scale carry the drama, so the camera is the part that most often goes wrong.

## What the source usually looks like

Few shots, long locked takes, the product small in a big frame, a three-quarter view for depth, a single big
moment (launch, flip, landing), the logo at the very end.

## References (in this order)

1. One **product storyboard** per shot (real frames, product swapped in) — or line drawings with the outline
   warning when no swap exists yet.
2. The **product character sheet** (front, three-quarter, side, back). Extra photos of the same product as
   PRODUCT VIEW, never PROP.
3. The **logo**, last, timed to the final 2 s.
4. Optional: the source as a VIDEO REFERENCE for camera and timing (strongest motion copy).

## Words that matter for this style

- SCENE CONTEXT: "a remix of an ad for a <old subject> with the <product> in its place"; the cut count.
- CAMERA: the distance and three-quarter position for wide shots; "static camera, no movement".
- ACTION TIMING: every movement step with its time (climb 1.7–7.4 s, launch 7.4 s, flip, thrusters, landing),
  the product's place in frame in brackets.
- The product can do things it physically can't (a mouse driving up a ramp). Say how: "glides on its base",
  "thrusters fire" — or the model invents wheels.
- LIGHTING · SOUND · STYLE: the source's light in words; music mood; "the <product> keeps its real materials".

## Results so far

| Run | What worked | What failed → fix |
|---|---|---|
| Line-drawing storyboards, product photo | Night look, smoke, launch | Car outline on the product; extra cuts → outline warning, one camera per shot |
| Hero still in the scene | Product look | Became frame 1; logo leaked → hero dropped, character sheet |
| Camera block + cut count | One locked far-back take after the cut | Side-on loop instead of three-quarter slab; a tripod drawn → three-quarter wording, "static camera" |
