# Style: Creator product reel (a compilation of one-shot product ads)

Back to [SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md). Status: **one remix run (2026-10-28), lessons from Sean's review; not yet a
finished, approved ad.** Update with evidence after more runs.

## How to spot it

- A creator's reel of separate mock ads, often captioned like "Creating fake campaigns for <brand> until they hire
  me". Many hard cuts, most shots 0.5–1 s stills, one or two moving shots, cut to the music.
- The scenes don't tell one story: each still is its own complete ad with its own idea.

## What makes it work (keep it in a remix)

- **The product is the star.** It fills most of the frame in nearly every shot; people, hands and props only frame
  it. A new idea where a person becomes the subject breaks the style.
- **Bold, big, brash.** Loud saturated colour backgrounds, big glossy close-ups, graphic set-ups (balloons, stacks,
  fanned rows, macro textures). New ideas stay in that studio world; never a quiet lifestyle scene.
- **One idea per shot, never repeated.** Each scene keeps its own concept for showing off the product.

## Remix rules for this style

- Judge each scene as its own ad: purpose → fit → new idea per shot (`../remix-workflow.md` step 4).
- Change only the prop or action that doesn't belong to the new product; the background, colour and scale stay.
- Each scene keeps its own product (flavour, variant) from the plan.

## Worked example: Baby Lips (lip balm) → Vilo Gelato 90 ml cups

| Source shot | Fits gelato? | New idea that kept the style |
|---|---|---|
| Hand picks a balm from a make-up display | No (wrong store) | Same hand and view, glass-front supermarket freezer |
| Balm in a jeans back pocket | No (frozen, too big) | Cup in an insulated cooler bag pocket, same colour background |
| Woman glides balm over her lips | No (applied, not eaten) | Big spoonful lifted to the camera, same background |
| Balm floating on water | No (melts) | Cup on crushed ice, beauty shot |
| Balms stacked, fanned, crossing on red | Yes | Same composition with the cups |

What went wrong first, so you don't repeat it: ideas moved to benches and cafés (lost the bold look), one flavour
in every scene (only the main product was named), shape and angle differences flagged as misfits (8 of 10 scenes),
and a stored swap kept the balm's clear cap floating next to the cups.
