- **tools:** Remotion or Blender + one reference still; the real clip

**Use when:** A motion ad must hand over to real footage (the founder, a customer, the product in use) with no
visible cut.
**Look:** The animation's last frame is exactly a given still; the real clip's first frame has the same composition,
so the cut disappears.

## Timing
- The last 0.5–1 s of the animation eases into the still (no motion on the final frames), then cut on a beat.

## Build
- Give one reference still (same framing as the clip's first frame). Instruct: "the end frame of the animation must
  be this still". Keep the background colour the same through the whole animation and into the clip.
- Check: diff the last animation frame against the still; then against the clip's first frame.

## Traps
- A different background between animation and clip shows the cut; match it, or pick a clip shot on that colour.
