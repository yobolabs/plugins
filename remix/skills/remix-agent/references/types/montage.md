# Type: montage

Back to [SKILL.md](../../SKILL.md). Load when the source is fast cuts of stills or short clips held on the music's beats.

- Scenes are mostly `still`: storyboard picture = the final picture, held for its window. No paid video for a held still.
- Every cut sits ON a beat; shots holding 2 beats go in the first half; the run into the end card is one shot per beat.
- Each picture used once; fewer assets than slots -> hold for 2 beats, never fill with a near-twin; never the same product in two shots in a row (reorder first).
- The ad's own music is the track (see `../join-and-sound.md`); no voices, so parts carry no audio.
- Slots under about 1 s merge into a neighbour.
- Merchant photo "as it is" is used unredrawn.
